About Nezeza Ijuru

Make heaven rejoice.

Nezeza Ijuru is the annual evangelism and discipleship mission of Zion Temple Kimironko. Since 2017, it has carried one enduring call: to see people reconciled to God through Jesus Christ, grow into mature disciples, and live lives through which Christ is revealed.

Our roots

A mission born from a wider ministry family.

Nezeza Ijuru did not begin as an independent initiative. It grew from a spiritual and ministry journey that begins with Authentic Word Ministries, continues through Zion Temple Celebration Center and Zion Temple Kimironko, and ultimately gave birth to the Nezeza Ijuru mission.

01

Ministry

Authentic Word Ministries

Founded by Apostle Dr. Paul M. Gitwaza, Authentic Word Ministries is the wider ministry family from which Zion Temple Celebration Center was established.

02

Church

Zion Temple Celebration Center

The church expression of the ministry, serving through different parishes and communities with a shared commitment to the Gospel and the Great Commission.

03

Parish

Zion Temple Kimironko

A local parish of Zion Temple Celebration Center where the vision for Nezeza Ijuru was initiated as an evangelism mission beginning within the local community.

04

Mission · Since 2017

Nezeza Ijuru

An annual evangelism and discipleship mission calling people to reconciliation with God through Jesus Christ, spiritual maturity, and lives through which Christ is revealed.

Why we exist

Reconciled to God. Growing in Christ. Revealing Christ.

Nezeza Ijuru exists to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, call people into reconciliation with God, nurture believers toward spiritual maturity, and encourage lives in which the character and fruit of Christ become visible.
