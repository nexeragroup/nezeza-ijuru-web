Our purpose

Make heaven rejoice by leading people to Christ.

Nezeza Ijuru means “Make Heaven Rejoice.” Launched in 2017 by Zion Temple Kimironko and inspired by Luke 15:10, it is an annual evangelism and discipleship mission calling people to be reconciled to God through Jesus Christ, grow into mature disciples, and live lives through which Christ is revealed.
